# 📋 STEP-BY-STEP DEPLOYMENT GUIDE

## Deploy: netlify-direct-fix.tar.gz → https://tsiemasiloportfolio.netlify.app/

### STEP 1: Download the Fix Package
- Download `netlify-direct-fix.tar.gz` from your Replit files
- Extract it to get the `netlify-direct-fix` folder

### STEP 2: Access Your Netlify Dashboard
- Go to https://app.netlify.com/
- Log in to your account
- Find your existing site "tsiemasiloportfolio"

### STEP 3: Delete Current Site (Important!)
- Click on your "tsiemasiloportfolio" site
- Go to "Site settings" (bottom left)
- Scroll down to "Delete site"
- Click "Delete site" and confirm
- This removes the old deployment that wasn't updating

### STEP 4: Create New Site
- Back on main dashboard, click "Add new site"
- Choose "Deploy manually"
- You'll see a drag-and-drop area

### STEP 5: Deploy the Fix
- Drag the entire `netlify-direct-fix` folder into the deploy area
- Wait for "Site deploy in progress" to complete (1-2 minutes)
- Your site will get a new random URL first

### STEP 6: Update Site Name
- In site settings, change the site name to "tsiemasiloportfolio"
- Your site will be back at https://tsiemasiloportfolio.netlify.app/

### STEP 7: Verify Changes
Visit your site and confirm you see:
- ✅ "UPDATED: Latest Projects Added" notice at the top
- ✅ Network Management System as first project
- ✅ AI Call Analyzer as second project
- ✅ WhatsApp: 082 806 9569 in contact section
- ✅ Coming Soon mobile apps highlighted
- ✅ All contact information visible

### TROUBLESHOOTING
If changes still don't show:
1. Clear your browser cache (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Try opening in incognito/private browsing
3. Check the browser console for any errors
