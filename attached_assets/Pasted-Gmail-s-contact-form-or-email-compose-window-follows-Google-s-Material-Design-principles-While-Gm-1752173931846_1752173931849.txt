Gmail’s contact form (or email compose window) follows Google’s Material Design principles. While Gmail doesn’t use a traditional “contact form” like websites do, its email compose box effectively functions as one. Here's a detailed breakdown of the style, colors, fonts, and borders used in the Gmail compose interface as of 2025:

🧾 1. Font
Font Family: Roboto, sans-serif (default across all of Google’s UI)

Font Size:

To field & Subject line: 13px – 14px

Message body: 14px – 16px

Font Weight: Regular (400) or Medium (500) for labels like “To” or “Subject”

🎨 2. Colors
Background:

Compose window: #FFFFFF (pure white)

Hover background: #F1F3F4

Text Color:

Labels (To, Cc, Bcc): #5F6368 (a muted gray)

Input text (you type): #202124 (very dark gray, nearly black)

Border Color:

Fields (To, Subject): #DADCE0

Send Button:

Background: #1A73E8 (Google Blue 500)

Text: #FFFFFF

🧱 3. Borders & Box Styles
To/Subject input fields:

Border Style: None visible normally (uses underlines only on focus)

On focus: Bottom border shows with active color #1A73E8

Compose box:

Border Radius: 8px (slightly rounded corners)

Shadow: Subtle shadow to give elevation (Material Design Elevation Level 4):

Padding: 16px all around within the compose window

Spacing between fields: 12–16px

🧩 4. Input Field Behavior
Focus state:

Light blue bottom border (#1A73E8)

Light blue glow/shadow

Placeholder text:

Light gray (#80868B)

Error state (e.g., invalid email):

Border or underline turns red (#D93025)

Helper text appears in red

📱 5. Buttons (Send, Formatting, Attach, etc.)
Icons: Material Design Icons

Icon Color (default): #5F6368

Hover State: Light gray background #F1F3F4

Border: None (icon buttons are typically circular with no border)

🧾 6. Layout
Max Width: Around 600px – 650px (responsive, adjusts slightly by screen)

Margin: Centered within the viewport

Z-index: High; appears over inbox UI (z-index: 2000+)
